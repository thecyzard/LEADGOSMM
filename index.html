Got questions?

Frequently asked questions.

Clear answers about services, orders, payments and account access—so you can make informed decisions before getting started.

01How does LEADGOSMM keep service prices competitive?
We aim to offer clear, competitive pricing by organising services in one catalogue and keeping service information easy to compare. There is no single fixed price for every platform or service. Check the live catalogue for each service’s price per 1,000, minimum and maximum quantity, and any applicable terms before placing an order. We do not promise to be the cheapest provider or advertise a universal starting price that may not apply to your selection.
02Is it safe to use LEADGOSMM services?
For standard SMM orders, you provide the public target link and the quantity—not your social media password. Keep your account credentials private and enable the security options offered by the social platform. Social networks may change their rules or systems, and no third-party engagement service can guarantee that an activity is risk-free or endorsed by a platform. Review the service description and the platform’s terms before ordering.
03How quickly will my order be completed?
Delivery time depends on the selected service, quantity, provider capacity and platform conditions. Each active service should display its estimated start or delivery time where that information is available. An estimate is not a guaranteed deadline. You can follow the order status from your customer workspace. If an order remains outside its stated estimate, contact support with the order reference.
04What payment methods can I use?
Available payment options are shown in the Add Funds area when they have been enabled for your account or region. Supported methods may vary by country and currency. A payment option should only be treated as available when it is displayed as active with its instructions. If no method is currently available, contact support before sending money—do not transfer funds to an unverified number or account.
05What is an SMM panel?
An SMM (Social Media Marketing) panel is an online dashboard where customers can browse social-media-related services, review service details and submit orders using a target link. LEADGOSMM brings supported platforms, service information, order tracking, wallet activity and customer support into one workspace. The services available depend on the active catalogue.
06Do you provide an API for resellers?
LEADGOSMM is designed to support approved reseller workflows, including service discovery, order submission, order-status checks and balance information through documented API endpoints. API access may require an application, admin approval and activation of the relevant integration. Keep API keys confidential, use them only on your server and never publish them in browser code or public repositories. Contact the team to request reseller access and confirm current availability.
07What does “refill” mean?
A refill is a service-specific option that allows an eligible order to be replenished if the delivered quantity drops within the stated refill period. Not every service includes refill protection. Check the service listing for its refill status, duration and conditions. If eligible, contact support with your order ID and the affected target link before the stated period expires.
08Can I cancel an order after placing it?
Cancellation depends on the service rules and the order’s current status. A request may be possible while an order is still pending, but it may no longer be possible once processing or delivery has started. Check the cancellation terms shown for that service and contact support promptly with your order reference. Please do not assume an order is cancelled until its status or support team confirms it. Any eligible refund is handled according to the applicable service and wallet terms.
iService availability, delivery estimates, refill and cancellation terms can differ by listing. Always review the current service details before confirming an order.
